Introduction

Join the Deployment and Operations team in the Communication Systems and Fabric Group and contribute to the reliable connectivity that supports one of the world’s leading scientific research environments.

You will coordinate and follow up telecom and network infrastructure works across CERN sites, working with contractors, users and technical teams to plan installations, check quality, ensure safety and maintain accurate documentation.
