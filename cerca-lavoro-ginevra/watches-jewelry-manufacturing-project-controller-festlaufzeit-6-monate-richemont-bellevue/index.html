Reference code: JR131796 Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity. BACKGROUND This role is part of a strategic project aimed at harmonizing accounting and financial practices within our W&J Maisons. Join this unique opportunity to: Actively contribute to the optimization of our processes and implementation of common standards Directly impact the efficiency and transparency of manufacturing finance. Contribute to a strategic and transformative project. Develop your skills in project management and process harmonization. Gain enriching and invaluable experience for your professional career, by collaborating with several W&J Maisons. HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT? Based in Bellevue and reporting to the Head of W&J Manufacturing controlling, your missions will include, but are not limited to: Drive Operational Excellence & Industrial Performance Lead monthly financial closing and reporting for the W&J industrial perimeter, providing analysis of operational highlights, cost structures, and manufacturing variances. Contribute to the consolidation and analytical review of financial data for budgets and reforecasts, ensuring alignment with Group guidelines. Proactively identify and implement process optimizations, streamlining workflows to enhance efficiency and automate manual tasks. Conduct ad-hoc financial and cost analyses to support strategic decision-making and team objectives. Ensure data reliability, quality, and automation within our data model, supporting digital transformation initiatives to optimize reporting tools. Contribute to Strategic Projects Participate in key projects focused on standardizing and harmonizing industrial costing definitions across all W&J production entities. Lead data collection and modeling to establish robust comparative benchmarks for Group manufacturing sites, focusing on operational efficiency, productivity, and cost competitiveness. Develop high-quality analytical slides and dashboards, fostering clear communication and alignment with diverse stakeholders across the Group and Maisons. This role involves punctual travel in Europe. HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US? Transversal skills Strong familiarity with project management methodologies and data collection processes. Highly developed analytical and synthesis skills. Flexibility and curiosity, with the ability to work autonomously and learn quickly. Dynamic team player, with excellent communication skills and ability to interact with diverse stakeholders, and challenge the status quo. Proactivity and initiative to propose improvement opportunities. Highly self-motivated, with ability to manage multiple deadlines Education, experience and core competencies University degree in Finance, Controlling, Accounting or similar. Minimum 5 years of solid experience in manufacturing/industrial controlling, audit, financial planning & analysis), or project finance. Strong understanding of manufacturing cost elements, standard costing, variance analysis, and industrial P&L structures. Expert level in Excel Strong knowledge of P&L cost structure based on standards costs and variance analysis. Experience in the W&J, Luxury, or high-end manufacturing sector is a plus. Hands-on experience with SAP (FI/CO) and consolidation tools as well as Power Query, Power Pivot, or Looker is highly valued Fluent in both French and English (written and spoken) is essential.
