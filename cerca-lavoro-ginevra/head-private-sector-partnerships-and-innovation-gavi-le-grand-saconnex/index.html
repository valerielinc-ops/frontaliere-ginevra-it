The Head, Private Sector Partnerships and Innovation (PSI), leads efforts to mobilise private sector resources and engage multinational companies and innovators in support of Gavi’s mission.

The role is responsible for developing and implementing private sector partnerships and innovation initiatives, brokering collaborations with stakeholders that can contribute to Gavi’s strategic objectives, and leveraging private sector expertise to address challenges across the immunisation landscape.
