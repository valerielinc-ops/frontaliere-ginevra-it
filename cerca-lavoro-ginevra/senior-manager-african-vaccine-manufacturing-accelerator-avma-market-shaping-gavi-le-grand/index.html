Responsabilità principali

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Lead the operationalization and ongoing implementation of the African Vaccine Manufacturing Accelerator (AVMA), ensuring delivery against programme objectives and alignment with Gavi’s regional manufacturing strategy.
  • Drive monitoring, evaluation, and learning activities for AVMA, generating evidence and insights that inform programme performance, strategic decision-making, and periodic course corrections.
  • Coordinate cross-functional collaboration across Gavi teams and Alliance partners to ensure effective governance, implementation, risk management, and stakeholder alignment.
  • Develop high-quality reporting, analyses, annual updates, and periodic reviews for Gavi governance bodies, donors, and other key stakeholders to support transparency and accountability.
  • Provide strategic and technical input into AVMA course corrections, reviews, and governance processes, contributing to recommendations and supporting endorsement through relevant decision-making forums.
  • Manage engagement with vaccine manufacturers and other external stakeholders, including planning, preparation, follow-up, and reporting associated with AVMA Manufacturing Forums.
  • Lead ecosystem analyses, manufacturing landscape reviews, and learning agendas that strengthen regional manufacturing planning, identify emerging risks and opportunities, and inform Gavi’s broader market shaping strategies.
